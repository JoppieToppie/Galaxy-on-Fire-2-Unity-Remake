source/Enterprise-D_Blender.blend is the original download (Blender 2.74).

Changes made for this project:
- Enterprise-D.fbx was converted from the .blend: the Mirror modifier applied, the Edge Split
  modifier baked into the normals, the interior set (Interior_Set) left out, the model turned so
  its nose faces +Z (Y up) and stored in centimetres. The procedural Cycles materials are only
  approximated (names, base and emission colours, texture links).
- The images that were packed inside the .blend are in source/textures/, unchanged.

ShareAlike: these converted files, and anything else made from this model, must be shared under
the same license. NonCommercial: not for commercial use without the author's permission.

In the game (ship 66, custom_ships.json), further changes:
- Enterprise-D.fbx is now split into one mesh per material (named by the material) and has an
  extra "Decals" mesh: copies of the hull polygons that carry the decal sheet, lifted slightly,
  with the decal UVs.
- textures/ now holds maps derived from the originals (hull albedo, metallic/smoothness, normal
  maps from the height maps, window emission, a brushed-metal detail pair, a white glow mask and
  the decal sheet at 2048 px). The original packed images are in source/textures/.
